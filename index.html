Our Heritage

A Legacy of Healing Since 1930

Welcome to Sarovaram Ayurvedic Backwater Health Center, where the timeless wisdom of 5,000-year-old Ayurveda meets a serene backwater retreat. Rejuvenate naturally with authentic herbal therapies and traditional Ayurvedic care.

Sarovaram Ayurveda is a trusted wellness center founded by Mr. Aykara Gopala Pilla Vaidyar and his wife, Ms. Thankamma. Rooted in traditional Ayurveda, it offers comfortable, naturally ventilated accommodation, authentic Ayurvedic medicines made in its GMP-certified facility, and personalized Panchakarma, wellness, and rejuvenation therapies under expert medical guidance.
